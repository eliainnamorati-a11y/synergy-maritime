Director of Operations

Panos Evangelou

Panos joined Synergy Maritime in 2014 and currently serves as the Director of Operations. He oversees the technical management and commercial operations of the fleet, ensuring the highest standards of safety, efficiency, and environmental compliance.

With a deep background in technical ship management and marine engineering, he has played a critical role in the group's transition toward sustainable, data-driven vessel performance and reliability.

He holds an MBA from the University of Nicosia and is an active member of several international maritime safety committees.
